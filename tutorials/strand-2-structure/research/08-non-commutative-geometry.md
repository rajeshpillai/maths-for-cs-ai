# Non-Commutative Geometry

## Mental

**Connes's non-commutative geometry (NCG)**: replace classical
spaces by **non-commutative algebras** (typically $C^*$-algebras),
viewed as "function rings of non-commutative spaces."

Motivation: many quantum-mechanical / mathematical-physics objects
*lack* a classical underlying space but are perfectly described by
operator algebras.

## Spectral triple

A **spectral triple** $(\mathcal A, \mathcal H, D)$:

- $\mathcal A$: $*$-algebra (the "coordinates").
- $\mathcal H$: Hilbert space (the "spinor sections").
- $D$: self-adjoint operator (the "Dirac operator").

Subject to compatibility axioms. Encodes a non-commutative
Riemannian-spin manifold.

**Connes's reconstruction theorem**: a spectral triple satisfying
classical axioms is equivalent to data of an ordinary spin manifold.
Generalises to non-commutative cases.

## Cyclic cohomology

The **non-commutative analogue** of de Rham cohomology. For an
algebra $\mathcal A$:

$$
HC^*(\mathcal A) = \text{cyclic cohomology}.
$$

Pairs with K-theory $K_*(\mathcal A)$ to give numerical invariants
(Chern character).

For commutative $\mathcal A = C^\infty(M)$: $HC^* = \bigoplus H^*_{\rm dR}(M)$
recovers ordinary de Rham.

## Worked example: non-commutative torus

$\mathcal A_\theta$: algebra generated by unitaries $U, V$ with
relation $UV = e^{2\pi i \theta} VU$.

For $\theta = 0$: ordinary torus $T^2$ functions.
For $\theta$ irrational: genuinely non-commutative; "non-commutative
torus."

Studied via $C^*$-algebraic methods. Has K-theory $K_0 = \mathbb Z + \theta \mathbb Z$
(rank-2 in $\mathbb R$).

## Symbolic

**$C^*$-algebras**: norm-closed $*$-subalgebras of $\mathcal B(\mathcal H)$.
Foundational objects of operator algebras.

**Von Neumann algebras**: weakly-closed $*$-subalgebras. Type I /
II / III classification (Murray-von Neumann).

**KK-theory** (Kasparov): K-theory equipped with bivariant
structure. Powerful for index theorems.

**Hochschild cohomology** $HH^*$: deformation-controlling cohomology
of an algebra; closely related to cyclic cohomology.

**Quantum Standard Model**: Connes-Chamseddine spectral-action
formulation derives Standard Model from NC manifold + Dirac op.
Speculative but striking.

## Computational

```python
import numpy as np

# Non-commutative torus generators (matrices satisfying UV = e^{2πiθ} VU)
# Realisation in N×N matrices for rational θ = p/N

N = 5
theta = 1/N    # rational, gives matrix realisation

omega = np.exp(2j * np.pi * theta)
U = np.diag([omega**k for k in range(N)])
V = np.eye(N, k=1) + np.eye(N, k=-(N-1))    # cyclic shift

# Verify UV = e^{2πiθ} VU
LHS = U @ V
RHS = omega * V @ U
print("UV - e^{2πiθ} VU max diff:", np.max(np.abs(LHS - RHS)))   # ~0

# This is the "fuzzy torus" — finite-dim approximation of NC torus
print("Non-commutative torus: realised as N×N matrices for rational θ.")
```

## Applied

- **Quantum mechanics** — operator-algebraic foundation of QM is
  inherently NCG.
- **Signal processing** — operator algebras model time-frequency
  analysis (Gabor frames).
- **Mathematical physics** — Connes-Marcolli on number theory and
  quantum statistical mechanics.
- **K-theory** — both K-theory and cyclic cohomology used as
  invariants of $C^*$-algebras.
- **Quantum information** — von Neumann entropy is precisely the
  NC analogue of Shannon entropy.
