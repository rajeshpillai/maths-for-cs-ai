## Three integrated walkthroughs

### Lurie program

Jacob Lurie's vast project (~2003-present):

- $\infty$-categorical foundations (HTT, HA).
- Spectral algebraic geometry.
- Cobordism hypothesis (extended TQFTs).
- Topological modular forms (TMF).
- Derived deformation theory (every formal moduli problem governed
  by derived Lie algebra).

Lurie reorganised much of modern algebraic topology and AG.

### Scholze-Clausen-Bhatt program

A cluster of recent breakthroughs:

- Perfectoid spaces (Scholze).
- Prismatic cohomology (Bhatt-Scholze).
- Condensed mathematics (Clausen-Scholze).
- Liquid Tensor Experiment formalised in Lean.
- Shrunken / pyknotic foundations.

Together: a new paradigm for $p$-adic and analytic geometry.

### AI-assisted research mathematics

**Lean's mathlib** + **AlphaProof** + **mathematicians**:

- mathlib has $> 10^6$ lines of formalised math, including parts of
  $\infty$-category theory, condensed math, and modular forms.
- AlphaProof solved IMO problems autonomously (Aug 2024).
- Polymath-style open collaborations using Lean / Coq are growing.
- Future: AI generates conjectures + proofs; humans verify and
  guide.

## Roadmap beyond Research-adjacent

There is no "beyond" — this *is* the research frontier as of 2026.
What's "next":

- New paradigms (condensed math, derived AG) gradually replace older
  foundations.
- AI proof systems play larger roles.
- Categorification continues at higher dimensions ($\infty$-toposes,
  $\infty$-operads).
- Cross-disciplinary connections (physics, CS, quantum) deepen.

## Closing

Algebra in 2026 is *the* organising language of modern mathematics:

- $\infty$-categories unify topology, algebra, geometry.
- Derived methods extend classical theorems to singular settings.
- Condensed mathematics fixes pathologies of analysis.
- Quantum groups bridge representation theory and physics.
- Non-commutative geometry generalises spaces.

Twentieth-century algebra dealt with classical equations; modern
algebra deals with **higher coherence and derived structure**.

The research-adjacent tier surveys where humans + AI are pushing
the boundary of mathematical knowledge.
