## Three integrated walkthroughs

### Mirror symmetry meets geometric Langlands

Kapustin-Witten 2007: gauge-theoretic interpretation of geometric
Langlands as 4d TQFT.

- Hitchin moduli on a Riemann surface = $\mathcal N = 4$ super
  Yang-Mills compactified.
- Mirror symmetry of Hitchin moduli (Hausel-Thaddeus) realises
  Langlands duality.
- Connects mirror symmetry, gauge theory, and geometric Langlands —
  three of the deepest 21st-century mathematical programs.

### Perelman + geometric analysis = topology

Hamilton-Perelman Ricci flow with surgery resolved Poincaré +
Thurston geometrisation. Modern follow-ups:

- Sphere theorem (Brendle-Schoen 2007).
- Differentiable sphere theorem.
- Yau closed-minimal-hypersurface conjecture (Marques-Neves +
  Liokumovich + Song).
- Synthetic Ricci on metric-measure spaces (Lott-Villani-Sturm).

### TDA + manifold ML = applied frontier

Modern ML increasingly uses geometric structure:

- Persistent homology features (Lesson 07).
- Optimal transport for distribution matching (Lesson 08).
- Equivariant neural networks (Strand 3 Master).
- Ricci-flow-style smoothing on data manifolds (Lesson 05).
- Hyperbolic embeddings for hierarchies (Strand 3 Master).

This bridges pure geometry to scalable engineering.

## Roadmap beyond Research-adjacent

The frontier moves; future directions:

- $\infty$-categorical foundations spreading throughout geometry.

  geometric theorems).
- Cross-disciplinary geometric methods (computational, physical,
  data-driven).

  productive.

## Closing

Geometry in 2026 is:

- **Frontier-rich**: mirror symmetry, geometric Langlands, persistent
  topology, synthetic Ricci all open / active.
- **Physics-coupled**: gauge theory ↔ geometric Langlands ↔ mirror
  symmetry tightly intertwined.
- **AI-augmented**: formal verification + AI-discovered conjectures
  becoming routine.
- **Engineering-relevant**: TDA, OT, equivariant ML, manifold ML use
  these ideas at industrial scale.

Geometry started as land surveying. Today it underwrites general
relativity, deep learning, persistent topology of complex data, and
the gauge-theoretic structure of fundamental physics — and the
frontier is rich.
