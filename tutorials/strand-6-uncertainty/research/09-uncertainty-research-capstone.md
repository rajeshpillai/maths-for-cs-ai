# Capstone — Uncertainty at the Frontier

## Explain Like I Am 7

This grand finale walks the frontier: random conformal SLE curves,
quantum-gravity crumpled surfaces, universal eigenvalue gaps,
free-probability semicircles, generalisation bounds for deep
learning, sample-efficient reinforcement learning, Bayesian
deep nets that know what they don't know, automatic causal
discovery, and the homogenisation of randomness into smooth
effective laws.  We thread these into three modern stories — image
generation, learning theory, and multi-scale modelling — to show
how researchers in 2026 actually use this entire toolbox at once.

## Three integrated walkthroughs

### Universality across 2D random systems

A unified narrative:

- **SLE** describes critical 2D interfaces.
- **LQG** is the random metric carrying these interfaces.
- **Random matrices** exhibit Tracy-Widom universality.
- **KPZ universality** (Strand 4 Research) connects polymer / TASEP /
  LIS to Tracy-Widom.
- **Free probability** captures asymptotic spectra.

A *universality web* spanning probability, mathematical physics,
combinatorics.

### AI theory: scaling, generalisation, alignment

Modern DL theory:

- **Approximation** (Strand 4 Research): what NNs *can* compute.
- **NTK / feature learning** (Strand 4 Research): how training works.
- **Scaling laws** (Strand 4 Research): empirical compute / data /
  parameters.
- **PAC-Bayes / SLT bounds** (Lesson 04): generalisation
  guarantees.
- **RL theory** (Lesson 05): sequential decisions.
- **Bayesian DL** (Lesson 06): uncertainty / robustness.
- **Causal AI** (Lesson 07): from correlation to causation.

Threads of contemporary AI theory.

### Multi-scale modelling

Modern scientific computing combines:

- **Stochastic PDEs** (Strand 4 Research).
- **Stochastic homogenisation** (Lesson 08): macro from micro.
- **Optimal transport** (Strand 6 Master): distribution matching.
- **Random matrices** for high-dim covariance.
- **MCMC** for Bayesian posteriors.

Climate, biology, materials all use combinations of these.

## Closing

Probability in 2026 is *the* mathematical framework for:

- **AI / ML** (training, sampling, generation, generalisation).
- **Mathematical physics** (KPZ, SLE, LQG, random matrices).
- **Scientific computing** (multi-scale, stochastic).
- **Causal data science** (intervention, counterfactual).
- **Decision theory** (Bayesian, robust, distributional).

The strand began with coin flips; it ends at the frontier where
probability, ML, mathematical physics, and AI safety converge.
